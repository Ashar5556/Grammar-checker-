Bad grammar in emails costs you more than you think

You've spent time writing an email. You hit send. Then you spot it — a typo, a missing comma, or a sentence that came out completely wrong. A grammar mistake in a job application email can get you filtered out before anyone reads your resume. That's where an email grammar checker helps.

What is an email grammar checker?

An email grammar checker automatically reviews your email text for grammar errors, spelling mistakes, punctuation issues, and tone problems. It suggests corrections in real time so your email reads clearly and professionally.

Before vs After: what an email grammar fix looks like

📧 Job application: "openning" → "opening" / professional subject line
📧 Client follow-up: "I sended" → "I sent" / polite tone
📧 Academic email: "wasnt, last day" → "wasn't, yesterday" / respectful

How to check grammar in an email (step by step)
